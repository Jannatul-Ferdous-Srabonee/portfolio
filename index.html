A note on this site

This portfolio is built to WCAG 2.2 AA as a baseline, tested with keyboard-only navigation and a screen reader, and every data visualization here ships with an accessible table fallback. The badge in the corner shows, in real time, whether the site currently thinks you're navigating by mouse or keyboard — try pressing Tab a few times.

Currently

Postdoctoral researcher studying screen-reader navigation efficiency in data-dense interfaces, and collaborating with two accessibility teams on component-level design guidance. Open to research scientist and UX research roles.
